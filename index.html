CEPA Terms

Contractor Equity Participation Agreement — key points

At-Cost Billing

Materials & labor invoiced at direct cost only — no markup, no cash risk to contractor.

Sweat Equity Formula

Forgone labor margin converts 1:1 into equity units at close, valued at ARV-based project equity.

Class B Units

Non-voting, profit-participating units entitling holder to pro-rata refinance and sale proceeds.

Graduation Clause

After 2 qualifying deals, contractor may elect direct title ownership via Contractor Holding Trust.
