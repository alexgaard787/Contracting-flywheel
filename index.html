Workflow

Four stages, one rolling system.

The Remodel (At-Cost)

The contractor covers labor and materials at cost through the operating S-Corp. The Property LLC issues Class B equity equal to the sweat-equity value created by the renovation and the schedule discipline behind it.

The Vouchers (Stabilization)

The Housing OpCo master-leases the site, captures Travis County and TDCJ voucher streams, and stabilizes revenue through recurring room-based operating distributions that do not depend on one-time retail closings.

The Refinance (Rolling Capital)

A commercial cash-out refinance returns the initial capital tax-free. Rolling Cash-Out Equity, paired with the next active contract, becomes the down payment and collateral for Property 2.

Property 2 to N (Universal Expansion)

The S-Corp keeps executing covered contracts while A/R and distributions compound inside the Parent Trust structure. Each PropCo child LLC is its own asset cell, ready for the next rehab, voucher lease, and refinance.
