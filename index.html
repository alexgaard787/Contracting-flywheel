Workflow

Three-step operating model

The Remodel (At-Cost)

The contractor covers labor and materials at cost through the operating S-Corp. The Property LLC issues Class B equity equal to the sweat-equity value created by the renovation and the schedule discipline behind it.

The Vouchers (Stabilization)

The Housing OpCo master-leases the site, captures Travis County and TDCJ voucher streams, and stabilizes revenue through recurring room-based operating distributions that do not depend on one-time retail closings.

The Refinance (Payout)

A commercial cash-out refinance returns the initial capital tax-free, then distributes a cash-out profit to the contractor based on the operating and equity value created during the stabilization period.
