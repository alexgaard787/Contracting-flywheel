Liability isolation

Three entities. One protected flywheel.

Your contracting company does not need to become the landlord. Each entity has one job and one boundary.

01The Shield · Contracting S-Corp

Handles direct trade labor, payroll, contracts, and warranties. It keeps active construction liability away from the real estate equity.

02The Asset · Property LLC

Holds title to the real estate and issues your Class B non-voting equity. Value accumulates where the property lives.

03The Operator · Housing OpCo

Master leases the property, interfaces with county and state voucher programs, and pays monthly distributions to the Property LLC.

Build the first unit. Then let the unit fund the next one.

This is an operating framework for discussion with your attorney, CPA, lender, and local housing partners. Economics vary by project, underwriting, program eligibility, and final documents.
